Getting Started with
Your Angkor Server

From order confirmation to your first multiplayer login — follow this quick guide to configure your Minecraft server in less than 5 minutes.

STEP 01

Select Your Plan & Region

Visit the Minecraft Plans catalog and pick a tier based on your player size:

  • 1–4 Players (Vanilla / Bedrock): Sapling (1GB) or Sprout (2GB)
  • 10–30 Players (Survival SMP + 20+ Plugins): Sapwood (4GB) or Grove (6GB)
  • 30+ Players or Heavy Modpacks (ATM9, Cobblemon): Canopy (8GB) to Ancient (16GB)
STEP 02

Access the Game Control Panel

Within 60 seconds of checkout, you will receive your Pterodactyl Game Panel credentials. Log into your control panel dashboard, click on your server instance, and you will see your live CPU/RAM stats, server terminal, and file manager.

STEP 03

Choose Server Type (Paper, Forge, Fabric, Bedrock)

Navigate to the Startup tab in your panel. Under Server Version, select your preferred engine:

  • Paper / Purpur — Highly recommended for regular survival and plugin servers.
  • Fabric — Best lightweight performance engine for modded gameplay.
  • Forge / NeoForge — Best for large industrial, magical, and adventure modpacks.
  • Geyser / Bedrock — Allows mobile (iOS/Android), Windows 10, and console players to join.
STEP 04

Recommended Java Garbage Collection (Aikar's Flags)

All Angkor Hosting instances come pre-configured with Aikar's optimized G1GC flags to eliminate server garbage collection lag spikes:

-XX:+UseG1GC -XX:+ParallelRefProcEnabled -XX:MaxGCPauseMillis=200 -XX:+UnlockExperimentalVMOptions -XX:+DisableExplicitGC -XX:+AlwaysPreTouch -XX:G1NewSizePercent=30 -XX:G1MaxNewSizePercent=40 -XX:G1ReservePercent=20 -XX:G1HeapWastePercent=5 -XX:G1MixedGCCountTarget=4 -XX:InitiatingHeapOccupancyPercent=15 -XX:G1MixedGCLiveThresholdPercent=90 -XX:G1RSetUpdatingPauseTimePercent=5 -XX:SurvivorRatio=32 -XX:+PerfDisableSharedMem -XX:MaxTenuringThreshold=1
STEP 05

Start Server & Connect Your Players

Hit the green Start button on the console tab. Once the console outputs Done!, copy your server IP address (e.g. play.angkor.host:25565).

In your Minecraft game client, go to Multiplayer → Add Server, paste your server IP, and press Done to connect!

STEP 06

Installing Plugins & Scheduling Cloud Backups

To add plugins like EssentialsX, Vault, or LuckPerms, open the File Manager, open the plugins/ folder, upload the .jar files, and restart your server.

To safeguard your world against griefing or corruption, open the Backups tab and hit Create Backup.
