Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • Compliant with GDPR & Privacy Standards

1. Information We Collect

We collect only essential information required to deliver and secure your hosting services:

  • Account Credentials: Email address and username for Pterodactyl panel authentication.
  • Billing Data: Transaction references and payment status (we do not store full credit card numbers).
  • Server Logs: Technical connection data and DDoS telemetry for network defense and stability.

2. How Your Data is Protected

All communication with our website and control panels is encrypted via TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords and sensitive server keys are hashed using industry-standard cryptography. We do not sell or monetize personal customer data to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Server world files and database backups are retained for the duration of your active subscription plus a 7-day grace period following cancellation, after which they are permanently and securely deleted from storage arrays.

4. Contact & Data Requests

To request an export or complete removal of your personal information, please contact our data privacy officer at support@angkorhosting.store.

Your privacy and server data security are our top priorities. Questions? Contact us anytime at support@angkorhosting.store.
