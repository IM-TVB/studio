Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Angkor Hosting collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and hosting services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and billing details provided when you sign up.
  • Payment information: processed securely through our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: server resource usage, login activity, and support ticket history.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and device information collected automatically for security and analytics purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our hosting services.
  • To process payments and manage billing.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate service updates.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted third parties who help us operate our services, such as payment processors, cloud infrastructure providers, and email delivery services — only to the extent necessary for them to perform their function, and under confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and billing information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, tax, and security obligations. Server content is deleted according to our data deletion schedule after account termination unless otherwise required by law.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out at support@angkorhosting.com.
